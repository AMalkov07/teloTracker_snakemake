exactly what the evidence supports (elements the pipeline genuinely cannot tell apart
at this identity cutoff). Counted per sample because each day-0 reference has its own
assembly and therefore its own grouping; group labels (G1, G2, ...) are LOCAL to a
sample and are not comparable across samples.
